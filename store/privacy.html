Skylark Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026 · Lihai La LLC

Skylark is a proxy client for Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Linux, published by Lihai La LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: we never see your traffic, and the app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking code. The only data we hold is for Windows and Linux purchases, described below.

Your traffic and the VPN permission

Skylark uses Android's VpnService (on Android) or Apple's Network Extension framework (on iPhone, iPad and Mac) to create a local tunnel on your device. Depending on your mode and rules, it sends your device's network traffic either directly to the internet or through a proxy server that you add yourself (by link, QR code or subscription). We don't operate these servers and we never receive your traffic. Whoever runs your proxy server can see the traffic that passes through it, under their own privacy policy.

Skylark does not record, store or send your browsing history, traffic content or connection logs to us or anyone else. A short technical log is kept in memory while you are connected, so the app can work and show its status. If the proxy engine crashes, a technical crash log may be saved on your device to help diagnose the problem. Neither log is ever sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your servers, subscriptions, rules and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network requests the app makes

  • Subscriptions: when you add or update a subscription, the app downloads that URL from the provider you chose.
  • Country IP list: to send sites in your country direct, the app downloads a public list of your country's IP ranges from GitHub (or its jsDelivr mirror). The request includes only the two-letter country code. Your country is worked out on the device from your SIM, network or region setting.
  • Time sync: the app checks the current time with a public time server (ntp.aliyun.com), because some proxy protocols need an accurate clock.
  • DNS: domain lookups go to the DNS servers shown in Settings (by default Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1; in mainland China, direct lookups default to Alibaba's 223.5.5.5). You can change them.
  • Speed test: the latency test opens a short connection to each of your servers.

None of these requests include personal information, and none go to us.

Windows and Linux licenses

This applies only to the Windows and Linux versions, which are sold on lihaila.com. The iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android versions send us nothing.

  • Purchase: we keep your email address, license key, billing country and order references, so we can deliver your key and support you. Your IP address is checked at checkout and activation, to decline mainland China. The license records don't include it, but like every request to our website it appears in our web server's logs.
  • Activation: when you activate, and about every two weeks after that, the app sends your license key, a hashed device ID, your PC's name and the app version to lihaila.com, to enforce the limit of 3 PCs. Remove from this PC deletes that PC's record.
  • Emails: we use your email address only to send your key and messages about your order.

Permissions

  • VPN: required to route traffic as described above.
  • Camera: only used when you choose to scan a QR code. Images are processed on the device and are not saved or sent.
  • Installed apps (Android only): the per-app proxy screen lists your launchable apps so you can choose which use the proxy. The list never leaves your device.
  • Notifications (Android only): shows the connection status while Skylark is connected.

Payments

If you buy Skylark on Google Play or the App Store, the payment is handled by Google or Apple; on lihaila.com it is handled by Stripe. We don't receive your card details. Their privacy policies apply.

Children

Skylark is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Open source

Skylark is free software under the GNU GPLv3. Its source code is available at github.com/Lihaila/skylark, so anyone can check what it does.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: support@lihaila.com.

云雀隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日 · Lihai La LLC

云雀（Skylark）是一款适用于 Android、iPhone、iPad、Mac、Windows 和 Linux 的代理客户端，由 Lihai La LLC（下称"我们"）发布。本政策说明应用如何处理你的数据。简单来说：我们从不查看你的流量，应用也没有账号系统、统计分析、广告或跟踪代码。我们仅保存 Windows 版和 Linux 版购买所需的数据，详见下文。

你的流量与 VPN 权限

云雀使用 Android 的 VpnService（Android 版）或 Apple 的 Network Extension 框架（iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 版）在你的设备上建立本地通道。根据你选择的模式和规则，设备的网络流量会直接访问互联网，或经由你自己添加的代理服务器（通过链接、二维码或订阅）转发。我们不运营这些服务器，也从不接收你的流量。代理服务器的运营方可以看到经过其服务器的流量，并适用其自己的隐私政策。

云雀不会记录、存储或向我们及任何第三方发送你的浏览记录、流量内容或连接日志。连接期间应用会在内存中保留少量技术日志，用于正常运行和显示状态。如果代理引擎崩溃，设备上可能会保存一份技术性崩溃日志，用于诊断问题。这两种日志都不会发送到任何地方。

保存在你设备上的数据

你的节点、订阅、规则和设置保存在应用的私有存储中，只在你的设备上，从不上传。卸载应用即会删除。

应用发出的网络请求

  • 订阅：添加或更新订阅时，应用会从你选择的服务商下载该地址。
  • 国家 IP 列表：为了让所在国家的网站直连，应用会从 GitHub（或其 jsDelivr 镜像）下载你所在国家的公开 IP 段列表，请求中只包含两个字母的国家代码。所在国家在设备上根据 SIM 卡、网络或地区设置判断。
  • 时间校准：部分代理协议需要准确的时间，应用会向公共时间服务器（ntp.aliyun.com）校准时间。
  • DNS：域名解析发送到设置中显示的 DNS 服务器（默认是 Cloudflare 的 1.1.1.1；在中国大陆，直连域名解析默认使用阿里云的 223.5.5.5），你可以自行更改。
  • 测速：测速功能会与你的每个节点建立一次短连接。

这些请求都不包含个人信息，也都不会发送给我们。

Windows 版和 Linux 版许可证

本节仅适用于在 lihaila.com 销售的 Windows 版和 Linux 版。iPhone、iPad、Mac 和 Android 版不会向我们发送任何内容。

  • 购买：我们保存你的邮箱地址、许可证密钥、账单国家和订单编号，用于发送密钥和提供支持。结账和激活时会检查你的 IP 地址以拒绝中国大陆访问。许可证记录不包含该地址，但与访问我们网站的所有请求一样，它会出现在网站服务器日志中。
  • 激活：激活时以及之后大约每两周，应用会将许可证密钥、经过哈希处理的设备 ID、电脑名称和应用版本发送到 lihaila.com，用于执行 3 台电脑的限制。「从这台电脑移除」会删除该电脑的记录。
  • 邮件：你的邮箱地址仅用于发送密钥和订单相关信息。

权限

  • VPN：用于按上述方式转发流量。
  • 相机：仅在你选择扫描二维码时使用。图像在设备上处理，不保存也不发送。
  • 已安装应用（仅 Android）：分应用代理页面会列出可启动的应用，供你选择哪些应用走代理。该列表不会离开你的设备。
  • 通知（仅 Android）：连接期间显示连接状态。

付款

如果你在 Google Play 或 App Store 购买云雀，付款由 Google 或 Apple 处理；在 lihaila.com 购买则由 Stripe 处理。我们不会获得你的银行卡信息，适用其各自的隐私政策。

儿童

云雀不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集儿童的数据。

开源

云雀是采用 GNU GPLv3 许可的自由软件，源代码公开在 github.com/Lihaila/skylark，任何人都可以查看它的行为。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，我们会更新此页面及上方日期。如有问题，请联系 support@lihaila.com。
