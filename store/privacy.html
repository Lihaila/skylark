Skylark Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Lihai La LLC

Skylark is a proxy client for Android, published by Lihai La LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: we don't collect, see, or store your data. Skylark has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking code.

Your traffic and the VPN permission

Skylark uses Android's VpnService to create a local tunnel on your device. Depending on your mode and rules, it sends your device's network traffic either directly to the internet or through a proxy server that you add yourself (by link, QR code or subscription). We don't operate these servers and we never receive your traffic. Whoever runs your proxy server can see the traffic that passes through it, under their own privacy policy.

Skylark does not record, store or send your browsing history, traffic content or connection logs to us or anyone else. A short technical log is kept in memory while you are connected, so the app can work and show its status. It is never written to storage or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Your servers, subscriptions, rules and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Network requests the app makes

  • Subscriptions: when you add or update a subscription, the app downloads that URL from the provider you chose.
  • Country IP list: to send sites in your country direct, the app downloads a public list of your country's IP ranges from GitHub (or its jsDelivr mirror). The request includes only the two-letter country code. Your country is worked out on the device from your SIM, network or region setting.
  • Time sync: the app checks the current time with a public time server (ntp.aliyun.com), because some proxy protocols need an accurate clock.
  • DNS: domain lookups go to the DNS servers shown in Settings (by default Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1). You can change them.
  • Speed test: the latency test opens a short connection to each of your servers.

None of these requests include personal information, and none go to us.

Permissions

  • VPN: required to route traffic as described above.
  • Camera: only used when you choose to scan a QR code. Images are processed on the device and are not saved or sent.
  • Installed apps: the per-app proxy screen lists your launchable apps so you can choose which use the proxy. The list never leaves your device.
  • Notifications: shows the connection status while Skylark is connected.

Payments

If you buy Skylark on Google Play, the payment is handled by Google. We don't receive your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies.

Children

Skylark is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Open source

Skylark is free software under the GNU GPLv3. Its source code is available at github.com/Lihaila/skylark, so anyone can check what it does.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: support@lihaila.com.

你的流量与 VPN 权限

云雀使用 Android 的 VpnService 在你的设备上建立本地通道。根据你选择的模式和规则，设备的网络流量会直接访问互联网，或经由你自己添加的代理服务器（通过链接、二维码或订阅）转发。我们不运营这些服务器，也从不接收你的流量。代理服务器的运营方可以看到经过其服务器的流量，并适用其自己的隐私政策。

云雀不会记录、存储或向我们及任何第三方发送你的浏览记录、流量内容或连接日志。连接期间应用会在内存中保留少量技术日志，用于正常运行和显示状态，这些日志不会写入存储，也不会发送到任何地方。

应用发出的网络请求

  • 订阅：添加或更新订阅时，应用会从你选择的服务商下载该地址。
  • 国家 IP 列表：为了让所在国家的网站直连，应用会从 GitHub（或其 jsDelivr 镜像）下载你所在国家的公开 IP 段列表，请求中只包含两个字母的国家代码。所在国家在设备上根据 SIM 卡、网络或地区设置判断。
  • 时间校准：部分代理协议需要准确的时间，应用会向公共时间服务器（ntp.aliyun.com）校准时间。
  • DNS：域名解析发送到设置中显示的 DNS 服务器（默认是 Cloudflare 的 1.1.1.1），你可以自行更改。
  • 测速：测速功能会与你的每个节点建立一次短连接。

这些请求都不包含个人信息，也都不会发送给我们。

权限

  • VPN：用于按上述方式转发流量。
  • 相机：仅在你选择扫描二维码时使用。图像在设备上处理，不保存也不发送。
  • 已安装应用：分应用代理页面会列出可启动的应用，供你选择哪些应用走代理。该列表不会离开你的设备。
  • 通知：连接期间显示连接状态。

开源

云雀是采用 GNU GPLv3 许可的自由软件，源代码公开在 github.com/Lihaila/skylark，任何人都可以查看它的行为。

变更与联系
