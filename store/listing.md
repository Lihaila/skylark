## English (en-US) – default

**Title** (27)
Skylark: Smart Proxy Client

**Short description** (74)
Easy proxy client for your own servers. One tap, smart rules, no setup.

**Full description**

Skylark is the easiest way to use your own proxy servers on Android. Paste a link and tap Connect.

IMPORTANT: Skylark is a client app. It does not include any servers. You need a server or subscription from your own provider or your own setup.

★ ONE TAP TO CONNECT
A big Connect button, live speed and a timer. Turn it on or off from the Quick Settings tile without opening the app.

★ ADD SERVERS IN SECONDS
• Paste a share link from your clipboard
• Scan a QR code
• Add a subscription URL and update it with one tap
• Or enter a server by hand
Links shared from a browser or chat app open straight in Skylark.

★ WORKS WITH THE PROTOCOLS YOU USE
Shadowsocks, VMess, VLESS (including Reality and XTLS Vision), Trojan, Hysteria2, TUIC, AnyTLS and SOCKS5. Subscriptions in base64 link lists, Clash YAML and sing-box JSON formats.

★ SMART MODE
Sites in your own country connect directly, everything else uses your proxy. Skylark detects your country automatically, and you can change it. Global and Direct modes are one tap away.

★ YOUR RULES
Add rules by domain, keyword, IP range or country, set to Proxy, Direct or Block. A "Bypass mainland China" option keeps Chinese apps fast when you're abroad.

★ PER-APP PROXY
Choose which apps use the proxy: all apps, all except some, or only the ones you pick.

★ SPEED TEST
See the latency of every server and pick the fastest.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your servers and settings stay on your phone. We never see your traffic.

★ OPEN SOURCE
Skylark is free software under the GNU GPLv3, built on the sing-box engine. Source code: github.com/Lihaila/skylark

★ IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文, switchable in Settings.

Questions or feedback: support@lihaila.com
