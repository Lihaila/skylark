Skylark for Linux

Install:    ./install.sh
Open:       from your app launcher, or run: skylark
Uninstall:  /opt/skylark/uninstall.sh

Needs WebKitGTK 4.1 (install.sh tells you the command if it's missing).
Skylark is a client app and includes no servers: you need your own server or subscription.
Not available in mainland China. Questions: support@lihaila.com
